---
title: Find sources (Scout)
---
1. Read your assignment handoff. Write the question as 3-8 search phrasings, including synonyms and the opposing view.
2. Search broadly, then narrow. Prefer primary sources (original data, official documents, first-hand accounts) over summaries of them. Stop at the point of diminishing returns, not at a fixed count.
3. For every candidate record: title, author or owner, date, location/URL, how you found it, and one line on why it is relevant. Open it before listing it.
4. Rank: relevance, closeness to the primary source, recency, independence. Mark anything you could not open.
5. Emit a `data` handoff to `research.analyst` (summary: how many found, how many ranked top; Content: the ranked table; Attachments: saved copies if any). Emit a `question` to `research.lead` instead if the scope is ambiguous.
