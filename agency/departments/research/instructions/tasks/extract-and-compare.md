---
title: Extract and compare (Analyst)
---
1. Read the Scout's `data` handoff. Work from the top-ranked sources first.
2. For each source, extract only what bears on the questions: claim, evidence, method or basis, date, and the exact place in the source.
3. Build a comparison matrix: questions as rows, sources as columns, with agree / conflict / silent in each cell.
4. For each conflict, say whether it comes from definitions, dates, method or error. Do not resolve a conflict by picking the more convenient source.
5. List known, assumed and missing separately.
6. Emit a `data` handoff to `research.verifier` with the list of claims that matter (Needed from you: check these) and a `report` to `research.lead` with the matrix and the three lists.
