---
title: Archive and cite (Archivist)
---
1. Collect the final records named in the Lead's `assignment`: sources, findings, verdicts.
2. One record per source, named `<year>-<author-or-owner>-<short-title>`, with fields: title, author, date, location, retrieved-on, relevance, verdict, and the claims that cite it.
3. Make a citation string per record in one consistent format; put it next to every claim in the findings.
4. Update the index (record name, one-line description, job id). Keep old records; mark replaced ones, do not delete.
5. Check: every claim has a citation, every citation resolves to a record, no orphans.
6. Emit a `report` to `research.lead` listing the index path and any record that is incomplete.
