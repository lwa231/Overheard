---
title: Hand-off
---
All agents exchange work through handoff files of one schema (docs/handoff-schema.md). To pass work on:
1. Pick the kind: assignment, report, question, answer, data, decision or escalation.
2. Address it `<department>.<role>` (or `<department>.any`); the file name carries the routing tag, so the receiver can skip files that are not theirs without opening them.
3. Fill the fixed sections: Context, Content, Needed from you, Attachments. Keep the summary to three lines.
4. Between departments, only Leads send, and only to the other Lead. A worker who needs another department asks its own Lead.
The receiver reads, acts, then marks the handoff done. A newer handoff may supersede an older one.
